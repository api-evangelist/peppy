The employee benefits that help accounting and finance firms attract and keep talent are the ones that solve problems people can't fix on their own. That means fast, confidential access to specialist healthcare for the moments that most often push experienced people out: menopause, fertility treatment, pregnancy and parenthood, and neurodiversity.
